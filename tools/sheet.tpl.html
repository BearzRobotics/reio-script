RE_iO Script — typing sheet

hover a glyph
Typing by keyboard in the box above: 4 keys per glyph — column group letter (a–m, the 13 blocks left to right), column in group 1–7, row group 1–7 (top to bottom), row in group 1–9 (0 = 10th). Example: a111 is the top-left glyph. Codes are shown on the axes. Esc cancels a half-typed code; space and Enter work normally; Backspace removes the last glyph. Hover a glyph to see its place and manner of articulation (labels from Canepari's Natural Phonetics & Tonetics, fig 10.1: columns = 63 places of articulation in 13 families, rows = 18 manners in 7 groups, each as a voiced/voiceless pair; the voiced-first order within a pair is inferred). Blue glyphs are sounds actually found in human speech (from chart 16180); the rest are the remaining possible combinations.
